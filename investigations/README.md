# investigations/

Triage writeups in a case-style format: what the alert said, what I did, what I concluded, what I'd escalate. This mirrors real SOC shift reporting.

## Template

```markdown
# Case: <alert title>

## Alert
What fired. Screenshot or raw text.

## Initial triage
What I checked first and why.

## Evidence gathered
Logs, artifacts, timeline.

## Verdict
Benign / Suspicious / Malicious + one-line reason.

## Escalation
What I'd hand to Tier 2 and why.

## Lessons
What I'd do faster next time.
```
